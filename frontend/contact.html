Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How does the skill recommendation score work?

Our algorithm matches the technical skills you add in your student profile against the required skills posted by the employer. The match percentage reflects how many required qualifications you possess.

Q: What file formats are accepted for resume upload?

CareerConnect strictly accepts Adobe PDF documents (.pdf) up to 5MB in file size to maintain presentation consistency and security.

Q: Can an employer edit job requirements after posting?

Yes, employers can edit any of their active job listings or close them at any time from their "Manage Jobs" dashboard.

Q: How do I test the administrator account?

The initial administrator is provisioned via the database seed script. You can log in using admin@careerconnect.com and password adminpassword123.
